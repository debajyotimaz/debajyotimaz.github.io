I work on code-mixed NLP, within the broader areas of multilingual and low-resource NLP.

I'm a Ph.D. scholar in Data Science and Engineering at IISER Bhopal, advised by Dr. Akash Anil. My Ph.D. focuses on the synergy between the code-mixed setting and its constituent native languages.

I have published in transactions such as ACM TALLIP, conferences such as EMNLP, WSDM and AACL-IJCNLP, and workshops including DHOW-MiLLA@TheWebConf. My industry experience spans LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning at NielsenIQ, and recommender systems at Adobe.
